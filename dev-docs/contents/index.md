# Documentation

The development documentation of Another Mood itself. It has three families: concept (the product vision and the core design decisions), design, and the development conventions and procedures.

The conventions every change needs, and the pointers to the rest, are in `DEVELOPMENT.md`; the conventions and procedures you open for a particular occasion are the dev family. For the source of truth of each kind of information, see the "Documentation" section of `DEVELOPMENT.md`.
