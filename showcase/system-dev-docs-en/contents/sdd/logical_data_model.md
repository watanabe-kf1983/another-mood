The entities without a corresponding term are the line entities ([Delivery line](node:/sdd/data_design/logical_data_model/delivery_line), [Sale line](node:/sdd/data_design/logical_data_model/sale_line), [Stocktake line](node:/sdd/data_design/logical_data_model/stocktake_line)), which the language of the business treats as part of their parent.

[Sale](node:/sdd/data_design/logical_data_model/sale) and [Sale line](node:/sdd/data_design/logical_data_model/sale_line) are never registered from a screen, because sales enter as notifications from the payment service. The only screen that refers to the two is the [Pending sale review screen](node:/sdd/user_interface_design/SC08), used when a notification includes an unregistered book and becomes pending. The [Book history screen](node:/sdd/user_interface_design/SC07), which shows the same two, defines no references for its items because it folds the three kinds of event, deliveries, sales and stocktakes, into one history table.
