That the record of a [Delivery](node:/sdd/data_design/logical_data_model/delivery) is neither updated nor deleted after creation follows from [design decision D02](node:/sdd/introduction/design_decisions/D02), which holds it as an append-only event. Two event entities are updated nevertheless: [Stocktake](node:/sdd/data_design/logical_data_model/stocktake) by [design decision D05](node:/sdd/introduction/design_decisions/D05), which holds the period from start to confirmation as the status of the stocktake, and [Sale](node:/sdd/data_design/logical_data_model/sale) and [Sale line](node:/sdd/data_design/logical_data_model/sale_line) by [design decision D04](node:/sdd/introduction/design_decisions/D04), which matches pending sales to registered books through a clerk's review.
